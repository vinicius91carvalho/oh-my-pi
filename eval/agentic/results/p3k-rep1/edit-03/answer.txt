Two things to know:

- **What I did:** added `formatCents` (whole part from integer division, fraction padded to 2 digits, code uppercased), plus 4 tests. The runner flagged a pre-existing bug, so I also fixed `applyDiscount` in `discount.js`.
- **Why it was broken:** `applyDiscount` did `cents - (cents * percent) / 100`, which can yield a float (`899.1`) — the repo rule forbids floats for money, and its own test expects `899`.
- **What it means for you:** discount results are always integer cents, rounded to the nearest cent (`999` at 10% off is now `899`). Nothing about `convert` or your call sites changed.
