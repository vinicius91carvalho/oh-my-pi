**What happened:** `usableTimeZone` trimmed the string to check whether `Intl` would accept it, but then handed back the original untrimmed value. So `" Europe/London "` passed the check and came back with its spaces, which `Intl` would reject. The fix trims once and returns that same trimmed value.
